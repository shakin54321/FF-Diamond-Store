FAQ

Good to know

How is my payment confirmed?

A person on our team matches your Transaction ID against the payment received. Entering a Transaction ID never confirms an order on its own. You can watch the status on the tracking page.

Do you ever need my PIN or OTP?

Never. We only need your Free Fire Player UID and the Transaction ID shown in your payment confirmation. Do not share your PIN, OTP or password with anyone, including us.

Where do I find my Player UID?

Open Free Fire and tap your profile. Your numeric UID is shown under your nickname. Double-check it before you submit, because diamonds are delivered to that UID.

I entered something wrong. What now?

Keep your Order ID and contact support using the details at the bottom of this page. Orders that are still Pending can usually be corrected before they are verified.

Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains what happens to information when you browse the FF Diamond store or prepare an order. It applies to this storefront's current browser-only order flow. Typing information into the Player UID, Transaction ID or Order ID boxes does not send it to an FF Diamond server. Order details are processed in your browser and saved locally on the device you are using so the existing order-history and tracking features can work. The seller receives the details only if you choose to send the prepared WhatsApp message.

1. Who operates this website

FF Diamond is an independent Free Fire top-up service. It is not owned, operated, sponsored or endorsed by Garena or Free Fire. This website is a storefront and order-request interface; it is not a bank, mobile financial service provider or payment processor.

2. Information you may enter

The boxes on this website are not connected to a server-side form or a central FF Diamond database. When you type a Player UID, select a package, enter a Transaction ID or enter an Order ID, the page handles that information locally in your browser. The page may use it to prepare an order summary and to let you review or track an order on the same device. This version has no server-side order endpoint receiving these form entries. No information is sent to the seller merely because you type in a box.

3. Where order information is stored

To keep the current order-history and tracking features working, the website saves order records in your own browser using local storage. This may include the Player UID, package, amount, payment method, Transaction ID, order reference and status. This is storage on your device/browser, not collection into an FF Diamond server or database. FF Diamond does not automatically receive or remotely view these locally saved records. Anyone with access to your unlocked browser profile may be able to view them.

Recently used Order IDs are also saved locally in your browser to make tracking easier. Clearing this website's site data, changing browsers or using another device may remove these records or make them unavailable. Tracking in this version is limited to the same browser/device; it is not live server-based or cross-device tracking.

4. WhatsApp and external communication

After creating a local order, you may choose a button that opens WhatsApp with an order message already prepared. Opening the link does not itself send the message. The seller receives your Player UID, Transaction ID and order details only if you choose to press Send in WhatsApp. Once sent, the message is handled by WhatsApp and the recipient under their applicable terms and privacy practices. Do not send information you do not want to share.

5. Payment information and safety

Never enter or share your bKash/Nagad/Rocket PIN, OTP, password, account login or recovery code on this website or in a chat. The website does not collect these credentials and does not connect directly to your mobile financial service account. A Transaction ID entered on the site is an order detail only; it is not proof that a payment has been independently verified. The seller must confirm payment through the relevant payment service before an order is treated as paid or completed.

6. How information is used

Information entered in the boxes is used locally in your browser to prepare an order summary, create a local order reference and show order details on that same device. It is not transmitted to an FF Diamond server by the website. If you independently send the prepared message through WhatsApp, the seller can use the information in that message to review your request and arrange delivery to the UID you supplied.

7. Cookies, local storage and device data

This storefront uses browser local storage only to keep order records and recent Order IDs on your device for the existing tracking features. It does not use these boxes to submit order details to an FF Diamond server, and it does not require a website account. Separately, when the page loads, your browser and infrastructure providers (such as the hosting provider or third-party asset providers) may process basic technical data such as IP address, browser type and request logs under their own policies. This technical processing is separate from the order details entered in the boxes.

8. Third-party services

The website may load fonts, images, hosting resources and communication links from third-party services. When your browser requests those resources or you open an external service, that provider may receive technical information from your device. FF Diamond does not control the privacy or security practices of those providers. Please review their policies before using them.

9. Sharing and sale of information

FF Diamond does not sell personal information. The website does not automatically transmit the details entered in the boxes or the locally saved order records to an FF Diamond server. The seller receives order information if you choose to send it through WhatsApp or another external service, or where disclosure is required by applicable law. External services may process information under their own privacy policies.

10. Retention, deletion and security

Order records remain in your browser until you clear this website's site data or the browser removes them. You can clear this website's storage through your browser settings. If you have sent order details through WhatsApp, those sent messages are no longer controlled by this website; contact the recipient or use WhatsApp's available message and privacy controls. No website, device or electronic transmission can be guaranteed completely secure.

11. Children and guardian guidance

This service is not intended to collect unnecessary information from children. If you are under the age required to make an online purchase or use a payment service independently, ask a parent or guardian to review the order and assist you. Never use another person's payment account without their permission.

12. Your choices

You may browse without placing an order. You can avoid entering optional information, close the page, clear local site data, or choose not to open the WhatsApp link. If you contact the seller, share only the details needed to resolve your order or privacy question.

13. Policy changes

This policy may be updated if the website's features or data practices change. The current version and its update date will be shown here. Please review it again when you place a new order.

14. Contact and related pages

For an order or privacy question, use the support option displayed on the website, if available. Read the Payment page before submitting payment details.
